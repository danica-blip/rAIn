Our story

Nobody opens a business to do paperwork.

Hi, I’m D. As a Director of Product, I saw how big companies use smart systems and AI to take the grind out of running a business. At home, I watched my husband pour his heart into our Muay Thai gym, and lose hours every week to scheduling, payments and messages.

Most small business owners start with a passion: a sport, a craft, a love of good food. Running the business is the hard part. So I started rain co. to bring big-company know-how to the local shops that deserve it most.

Our mission

We help small business owners use AI as a helper, not a replacement, so they spend less time on busywork and more time on what they love. The hardest workers deserve the biggest benefit.
